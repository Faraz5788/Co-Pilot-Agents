How a release moves

Four stages.
No heroics.

01 / 04

Build once, sign it.

Every image is built from the commit, signed, and pinned by digest. What you tested is exactly what runs.

Canary at five percent.

A slice of real traffic reaches the new version first, in one region, while the old version carries the rest.

Ninety seconds of attention.

Harbor compares error rate and p99 latency against the previous release. Nobody has to stare at a dashboard.

Promote, or quietly undo.

Healthy releases roll to every region. Unhealthy ones are pulled back in under four seconds, and you get a note explaining why.

# Ramadan Companion: notes for the buyer guide and listing

- **Unlock code:** IFTAR-8M3Q (website demo → full app).
- **Prayer times guide section must say:**
  - Times are calculated on the phone for the chosen city; each country's usual method is picked automatically.
  - UK buyers: many mosques use **Moonsighting Committee (UK)**. Choose it in Suhoor & Iftar → Calculation method.
  - To match your own mosque's timetable exactly, use **Suhoor & Iftar → Adjust times (minutes)** to nudge any time earlier (−) or later (+). If the app and your mosque differ, follow your mosque.
  - Set the **first day of Ramadan** to the day your community starts (moon sighting differs); default is 8 Feb 2027 (Umm al-Qura).
- **Accuracy checks done:** matches the adhan library within ~1 min (14,040 times, 13 cities, 6 methods; Moonsighting Committee 21,900 times across UK cities, all of 2027). Diyanet (Istanbul) and Kemenag (Jakarta) safety minutes built in and checked against published timetables. Fajr rounded down, Maghrib rounded up.

## Story

Hello, and welcome to The Geek Next Door Co.

I'm the friendly geek next door: the one people ask to "just sort out a spreadsheet" or set up an app. I started this shop to make those same clever tools for everyone, and to make them simple enough to open and use straight away, with no tech skills needed.

Everything here is digital, so it arrives the moment you buy. There's nothing to post and nothing to wait for.

**What you'll find here:**

🎄 **Christmas HQ.** One app for gifts, budgets, Secret Santa, gift tags and the Christmas countdown, plus colouring and activity printables for the kids.

🕋 **Umrah Companion.** A step-by-step Umrah guide with Tawaf and Sa'i counters, duas in Arabic with pronunciation and meaning, a Madinah guide, ziyarat and everyday Arabic phrases, in 7 languages.

🌙 **Ramadan Companion** *(coming soon)*. Suhoor and iftar times for your city, a 30-day tracker, a Qur'an plan, a zakat calculator and an Eid planner, in 7 languages.

📊 **Budget and life planners.** Ready-made spreadsheets for budgets, bills, savings, debt payoff, trips and goals that work in Excel and Google Sheets.

🖍️ **Kids' printables.** Colouring books, activity packs and party games you can print at home as often as you like.

**How I make things:**

• **Easy to use.** Clear layouts, plain instructions and a short guide with every download.
• **Works on your devices.** The apps run on iPhone, Android, Windows, Mac and Chromebook, with no account and no subscription.
• **Private.** Your lists and progress stay on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
• **Made with care.** For the Islamic apps, every dua shows its source, and where scholars differ I say so and suggest asking a scholar you trust.

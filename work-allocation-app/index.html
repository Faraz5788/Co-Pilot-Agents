Work Allocation Fee earner matching

New piece of work

Describe the work. The app checks every fee earner's skills, absence and current utilisation over the work period and ranks who is best placed.

Fee earners

Grade, practice group, chargeable target, current utilisation and skills.

Record absence

Annual leave, sickness, training, secondment etc. Weekends are ignored automatically; bank holidays can be added under Data & settings.

Absence log

My skills profile

Fee earners pick their name and keep their own skills up to date. Levels: 1 Working knowledge · 2 Experienced · 3 Expert.

Allocated work

Work assigned through this app. Allocated hours reduce each fee earner's free capacity in future searches.

Matching settings

How the ranking is weighted. Weights are normalised, so they don't need to add up to 100.

Utilisation ceiling: someone at or above this % of their chargeable target is treated as having no spare capacity.

Import from your systems

Upload CSV exports (e.g. from your time-recording system, HR system or Power BI). Download a template to see the expected columns. People are matched by name (or email if present).
